3 Framing performance

3.1 Mullion deflection
The Contractor shall limit the lateral deflection of any mullion under the
serviceability wind action to span/250 and to 15 mm absolute, whichever is
smaller. This requirement is more onerous than SSGC:2024 clause 5.2 and takes
precedence for this project.

3.4 Movement joints
Stack joint accommodation shall provide not less than 25 mm of vertical
movement per floor without loss of air or water performance.

7 Basis of design references
This specification is to be read with SSGC:2024 and the Region Q National
Annex SYN-NA. Where documents conflict, the most onerous provision governs
unless the Engineer directs otherwise in writing.

9 Items explicitly outside this specification
Seismic design, blast enhancement, and fire-rated curtain wall zones are not
covered by this specification and are subject to separate project documents
not issued at this revision.
