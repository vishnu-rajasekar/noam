Synthetic Structural Glazing Code SSGC:2024
(Fully invented non-proprietary fixture text. Not a real standard.)

1 Scope
This synthetic code gives fictional rules for unitised curtain wall framing
used only to test the Standards Scout harness. No value here is an engineering
requirement.

4 Actions and combinations

4.3 Wind action combination factor
The characteristic wind action W_k shall be multiplied by the combination
factor psi_w = 1.4 for the ultimate limit state, unless a National Annex
specifies a different value for the project location. The combination factor
applies to both positive and negative (suction) wind pressure.

4.6 Dead load of infill panels
The self weight of glass and opaque infill panels shall be taken as an
unfactored characteristic action G_k determined from the declared panel
build-up. A default areal weight of 0.30 kN/m2 per 6 mm glass ply may be
assumed when a project value is not declared.

5 Serviceability of framing members

5.2 Mullion and transom deflection limit
Under the serviceability wind action, the maximum lateral deflection of a
mullion or transom spanning between structural supports shall not exceed
span/175, and shall not exceed 19 mm in absolute terms, whichever is smaller.
For members supporting a corner or movement joint, the deflection under
serviceability wind action shall not exceed span/250.

5.4 Deflection under combined thermal and wind action
Where thermal bow is combined with serviceability wind action, the combined
lateral deflection shall not exceed span/150.

6 Structural sealant joints

6.2 Minimum structural sealant bite
The structural sealant bite dimension b_s shall be not less than 6 mm and not
less than the value obtained from b_s = W_k * a / (2 * sigma_des), where a is
the shorter supported edge length of the pane and sigma_des is the design
tensile stress of the sealant declared by the manufacturer. The bite dimension
shall additionally never be taken greater than three times the sealant glue
line thickness.

6.5 Sealant glue line thickness
The structural sealant glue line thickness shall be not less than 6 mm for
two-side supported panes and not less than 8 mm for four-side supported panes.
